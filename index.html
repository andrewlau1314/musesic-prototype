One exercise, one next action, one parent note

A cleared exercise

The learner opens a short original line, at most 30 seconds, written for beginners at about Grade 1 to 2.

Pitch and onset only

The practice reading notes pitch and note onset, then gives one next action the exercise can support.

A weekly parent note

The note is in Cantonese and English. It lists time, completion, and any pitch or timing difficulty on those same readings.

What this pilot is for

The Year-1 proposal treats these as hypotheses about a Hong Kong parent who already pays for a weekly beginner lesson. They are not interview findings.

  1. The days before the next lesson need one task, not a pile of materials.
  2. A pitch or timing mistake should be written down before the next lesson, on that same exercise.
  3. A parent should be able to read the week in Cantonese and English. The weekly teacher still teaches.
